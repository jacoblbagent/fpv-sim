Arming is inhibited while the throttle stick is above idle, so a raised stick can't launch the quad. Keyboard throttle resets to zero on every arm/disarm.

Keyboard response scales how fast a held key ramps the stick and the throttle — lower is gentler. Ignored when a radio is connected.

The airframe is a 1S 65mm whoop (BetaFPV Air65 II class, ~25 g AUW): about 4:1 thrust-to-weight, hover near half stick, ~54 km/h flat out. Wind is a real breeze in m/s — drag is taken against airspeed, so a steady wind carries you downwind and you lean into it; 0 is indoor calm.
